## Feasibility
| Task | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Binary genuine vs synthetic alteration | Feasible with caveats | Alterations are random stroke edits of the same image, not forgeries |
| Different-signer (random forgery) rejection | Feasible | Other signers' genuine images are available within each split |
| Signer-specific model | **BLOCKED** | One genuine specimen per signer – no within-signer variation |
| Siamese / metric learning | **BLOCKED** | Same reason, plus no real skilled forgeries |
| Independent evaluation | Limited | Signer-disjoint splits exist, but positive pairs must be simulated |

Synthetic re-captures of one signature are **not** independent genuine specimens and are reported as
simulated positives everywhere they are used.
