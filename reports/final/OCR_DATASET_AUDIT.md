## Interpretation
- This is a collection of cheque images, not a labelled handwriting dataset. No text transcriptions were
  invented.
- Usable weak labels: bank, split, cheque number (filename). The cheque number labels allowed training
  and testing an E-13B MICR digit reader.
- Account numbers printed on the cheques match the signature-dataset filenames for the same bank, which
  gives an independent cross-reference for the account field.
- Shared templates across splits mean layout-based extraction will look better on this test split than on
  cheques from unseen templates.
